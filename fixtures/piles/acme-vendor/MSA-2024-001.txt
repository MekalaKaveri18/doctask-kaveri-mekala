MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Document-ID: MSA-2024-001
Effective date: 2024-01-01
Buyer: Northwind Procurement LLC
Vendor: Acme Cloud Services Inc.

1. Parties
This Agreement is between Northwind Procurement LLC ("Buyer") and Acme Cloud Services Inc. ("Vendor").

2. Fees
Buyer shall pay an annual platform fee of USD 120000, invoiced in four equal quarterly amounts of USD 30000.

3. Payment terms
Invoices are due Net 30 from invoice date.

4. Term
Initial term is 24 months. The Agreement auto-renews for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives 60 days written notice.

5. Service levels
Monthly uptime commitment is 99.9 percent. Service credits are 10 percent of the monthly fee for each month below that commitment.

6. Notices
Notices to Vendor: legal@acme.example
Notices to Buyer: contracts@northwind.example
